The sounds of the village hall soon fade behind you as you carry on down Crow Arch Lane. The lane stretches south through this part of Brookdown Crossing, its cobbled surface busy with townsfolk going about their daily business. Shopkeepers tend their displays, children dart between the crowds, and laden carts rumble steadily along the street.

To the east, two alleys snake into the cluster of buildings. The first, a narrow passage, bears the name Moon Shiver Alley. Sheltered by tall buildings on either side, the alley lies in deep shadow, broken only by a strange silver glow that spills from somewhere deeper within. The second, Malt Way, is wider and brighter, but strangely less busy than Moon Shiver Alley.

You hesitate at the junction, deciding whether to remain on the lively thoroughfare or venture into one of the alleys.

With the farm far behind you, you reach a fork in the dusty road skirting the edge of town. To the left, Pilgrims Way will take you south and out of town. To the right lies Brick Lane, a well-trodden route heading west. It cuts across the fields, carving a path directly towards the clustered buildings that herald the entrance to the busier areas of town.

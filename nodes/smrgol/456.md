As boulders, bark, earth and stone rain around you, instinct takes over. You throw yourself backwards as an enormous chunk of earth and stone explodes in front of you. With no time to think, you roll again, this time finding the meagre shelter of an uprooted tree. You make yourself as small as possible as the destruction rages around you.

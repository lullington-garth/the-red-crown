You pause before stepping onto the path.

You take a deep, steadying breath, assessing the stones before you. You notice that, oddly, they appear to have fractured quite evenly. You look closer, taking in every detail: the tiny chip, the hairline crack, the shimmer of heat, the fire licking through the broken surface, and then you see it—a pattern.

Strange though it seems, while there are at least a hundred fractured stones ahead of you, they repeat in design, and of the various patterns of stone, only one traverses the full width of the fissure. It seems the floor itself is guiding you, if only you pay attention.

"Choose already!" growls Ommadon, but you will not be hurried.

You gingerly apply your weight to a stone on what you believe to be the safe path. It holds. To prove your theory, you tap a stone of a different pattern with your staff. It shatters and tumbles into the fire below. You choose the next closest stone that matches yours. It holds... then the next...

Almost without noticing, you find yourself on the final stone. You step onto solid ground. The fractured stones shimmer once, then settle into solidity as the fissure closes over.

You are through, unscathed.

Ahead, an enormous dragon squats before the entrance to a cave system, lazily biting chunks from the surrounding limestone and swallowing them whole. Its scales are a deep emerald green, and its broad snout is crowned with a pair of ivory horns. Golden scales cover its throat, chest, and belly, glittering whenever they catch the light.

When it has finished eating the cave front, it thrusts its head into the cave mouth and bellows, "Diamonds! Bring me diamonds!"

When nothing happens, it looks a little bemused and mutters, "It always works for the old man."

It tries snorting a stream of fire into the cave instead, and this seems to have the desired effect. A moment later, panicked dwarves come rushing out, carrying sacks overflowing with glittering gems.

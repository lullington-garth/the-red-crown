"Good!" says Gorbash, stamping a foot, the reverberations from which send you sideways. "Crush out the evil," he says. "It's the only way."

He scratches at a large rock at the bottom of the cave system.

"Here," he says, rolling the boulder to reveal a nook in the cliff face. "Take these." He points into the nook with a giant talon, and you notice a rolled parchment on top of which sits a pendant.

You collect the items and look them over. One is a scroll that can turn any item to gold; it might be useful. The other is a dark pendant, buzzing with magical energy and etched with a scythe emblem. You look up at the dragon.

"The Reaper's Blessing," he says. "Blocks death. Take it. It's good protection you'll hopefully never have use for."

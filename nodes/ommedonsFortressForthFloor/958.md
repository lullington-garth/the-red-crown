You set the acorn into the hollow.

The silver-blue lines unfurl, cradling the acorn like a newborn child. Their glow deepens until the entire chamber hums with quiet power.

The pool brightens, its surface becoming luminous. Within it, you see Peter smiling as he plays a game with a shopkeeper in what appears to be a pawnbroker's shop.

The pawnbroker watches as Peter collects all the pieces together and then places just the four wizard pieces on the board.

The pawnbroker looks surprised, but completely at ease with his decision.

Peter laughs softly. The vision dissolves into light, and the silver-blue threads gently release the acorn.

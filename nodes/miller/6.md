You raise your voice above the grind of timber and water. "Take care. Be careful with your machines, I say."

The mill door slams open. Three scrawny men emerge, sleeves rolled, faces sharp with irritation. One squints across the river, then barks a laugh. “What’s this? A hedge-wizard lost his way?” Another stoops, snatching up a stone. “Be gone, you relic!”

He hurls the rock your way. It skips wide, missing you by a second.

You stand your ground as the third man spits into the current. “Off with you, old fool,” he snarls, pulling his arm back and releasing the largest rock yet.

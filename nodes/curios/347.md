You quietly mutter the words of the spell, creating a perfect but non-functional copy of the ring. It smokes in your hand as it forms. When done, you quickly swap it for the ring in the cabinet.

The real ring is cold as you pluck it from its cushion. Even when held, it is still difficult to focus on as smoky shadows roll from it across your hand. The ring gives off a strange sense of companionship, but as to its use, you are none the wiser. You will only find that out by wearing it, and even then, that’s not a guarantee. The little brass plaque gives no clue: “The Ring of Shadow Slip” is all it says.

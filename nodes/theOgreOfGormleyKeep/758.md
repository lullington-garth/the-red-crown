By some miracle, you have bested the Ogre of Gormley Keep. You lie panting on the battlements, the ogre motionless nearby.

It is a long time before you are strong enough to move again. You eventually pick yourself up and make your way over to the hulking form of the fallen ogre. Dark, thick blood seeps from his wounds, pooling among the stones where he fell.

The blood of ancient magical creatures often possesses unusual properties, and few creatures are as strange as the Ogre of Gormley Keep.

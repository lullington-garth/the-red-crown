You raise the Shield of Saturn towards the darkening sky. The deeply etched runes blaze across its surface.

A golden barrier forms around you as a beam of brilliant light erupts upwards, tearing a hole through the black hand. It recoils in the sky, its fingers unravelling into smoke, the black plumes dissolving beneath the ferocity of the shield's light. As it does, you can feel the pervasive dark magic lift from your mind and flee back to its maker.

A thunderous cry echoes across the wasteland. Then the darkness is gone. The red, storm-lit sky returns—unpleasant, but still a blessed relief after the cloud of dark magic.

‘See them? No. I don’t keep ’em all together. Never know what magic ’ll do if you keep it all bunched up like that, and I’m not stompin’ all over the farm gatherin’ ’em up. I’ll tell you what I got, though, and you can take it or leave it. Only one, mind you. We ain’t a curiosity shop, and I’ve got work to be doing. Now what have I got...’

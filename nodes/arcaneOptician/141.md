The strange assortment of objects in the window bears little resemblance to the shop within. Stepping through the door, you find yourself in a lens crafter’s workshop.

Behind a bench sits a small man with a thick iron-grey beard. One eye is hidden behind a strange multi-layered eyepiece as he studies a glowing lens. After a moment, he sets it aside, wipes his hands on a stained leather apron, and looks up.

“Handle Loveacre,” he says with a slight bow. “Specialist Arcane Optician. What can I help you with?”

Without waiting for an answer, he reaches beneath the counter and produces a slim silver monocle resting on velvet cloth.

“Maybe this, the Monocle of Appraisal. A lovely little piece. It reveals the magical nature of any item you study through it. Can’t spot curses, mind you, but if it could, you probably couldn’t afford it.” He chuckles. “Fifteen Gold Pieces, if you’re interested.”

“I have other curiosities besides,” he says, gesturing to the countless glittering lenses around the shop. “Seeing lenses. Reading lenses. Hunting lenses. One or two I probably shouldn’t sell at all…” He chuckles again. “So then… what do you fancy?”

"There is little time for this," rumbles the gatekeeper, but he does not stop you.

You walk the courtyard, taking in the majesty of the great tree and the craftsmanship of the masonry that encloses it. Great and ancient stories hang frozen within the huge stone murals, marking the passing deeds of kings, queens, and gods alike.

Within all the stone grandeur, you spot a humble carved font. It is conspicuous in its understatement. A small, plain, roughly carved stone basin hewn into the wall, lacking any embellishment other than some crude animal depictions around its rim: a maggot and a fly; a tadpole and a frog; a caterpillar and a butterfly. The water looks fresh and clean, but you sense a great power emanating from the basin.

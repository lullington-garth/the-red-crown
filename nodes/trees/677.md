"So... nice windows," you say, looking up at what looks like quite a brutal battle between two armies of giants depicted in stained glass.

"Touch it," says the gatekeeper.

It seems like an odd instruction, but you follow it all the same, reaching up and placing your hand on the window. As soon as you make contact with the glass, you are swept into screaming, howling chaos. Your mind reels, trying to adjust. It takes you a second to realise that you are in the battle. Giants clash around you, tearing up trees as weapons, roaring like elephants, fighting with the fury of gods. A shadow falls over you. A giant tears across the battlefield. His foot comes down on you. You cower and scream...

You are back in the passage.

"Shall we?" says the gatekeeper, turning into the passage.

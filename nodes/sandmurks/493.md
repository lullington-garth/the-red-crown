Having finished the delicious lolly, you now find you are not sure what to do with the stick. It's too sticky (and useless) to put in your pack, but you also don't want to litter what feels like a hallowed place.

You hold it up to the gatekeeper and ask, "Err, what should I do with this?"

The gatekeeper looks at you for a long moment, then points at the ground near his feet. Unsure, you place the lolly stick at his feet. The giant then stoops and, with one finger, draws a circle around the stick. Instantly, it sprouts. Roots worm into the ground while shoots stretch up into the air, yawning as tiny leaves spring from thickening limbs. Within moments, it is knee height and glistening with silver, icicle-like fruit. It is beautiful.

You look up at the gatekeeper. He nods and beckons for you to follow him.

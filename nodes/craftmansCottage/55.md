The road is dusty and uneven, bordered by scrubland and pockets of wildflowers swaying in the cold afternoon breeze. Before long, you come upon a small Craftsman’s Cottage standing alone a little way back from the main path. Smoke curls from its chimney, and the sound of shouting carries through the air.

Outside the cottage, a group of men are fighting off a large grey wolf. The beast snarls savagely, growling and baring long yellow fangs as it snaps at the men.

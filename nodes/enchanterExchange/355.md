As you follow the road, a wash of strange smells that are difficult to place, and a feeling of crackling magic, drift through the air. A lively market opens up in front of you and, as you near it, you realise that it is an Enchanters' Exchange — a sprawling covered market where magic is traded as casually as fish at a dockside stall.

Robed figures haggle loudly over bottled enchantments while hooded scribes scratch notes into ledgers bound with chain. Sparks burst from worktables, curses hiss from locked jars, and somewhere deeper inside, people take cover as something explodes in green flame.

You pause at the threshold of the busy market.
